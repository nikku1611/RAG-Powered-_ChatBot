Machine learning is a branch of artificial intelligence that enables computers to learn from data and improve their performance without being explicitly programmed. It works by using algorithms and statistical models to identify patterns, make predictions, and take decisions based on past experiences. Machine learning is widely used in applications such as recommendation systems, image and speech recognition, fraud detection, medical diagnosis, and autonomous systems. By continuously learning from new data, machine learning systems become more accurate and efficient over time, making them an essential technology in modern data-driven industries.
